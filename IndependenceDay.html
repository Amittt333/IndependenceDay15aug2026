Dawn of Freedom

15 August 1947

On 15th August 1947, India awakened to life and freedom after centuries of colonial rule. As the tricolor flag soared over the Red Fort in Delhi, the world witnessed the triumph of fearless resilience, peaceful resistance, and united courage. Today, as we commemorate our journey towards 2026, we honor the immortal legacy of our ancestors and reaffirm our pledge to building an inclusive, progressive, and self-reliant nation.
